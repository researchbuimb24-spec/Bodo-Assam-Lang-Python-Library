Aronai Bodo sanskriti ka sabse pavitra aur aadarneey angavastra (scarf) hai.
Yeh prem, samman aur shubhkaamnaon ka prateek mana jaata hai. Aronai ke dono
kinaron par Bodo sanskriti ke paramparik motifs aur Agor bune hote hain. Kisi
bhi atithi ke swagat, guni vyaktiyon ke samman (felicitation), tyohaaron
(jaise Bwisagu), aur paramparik nritya (jaise Bagurumba) mein ise sammanpoorvak
gale mein pehnaya jaata hai.

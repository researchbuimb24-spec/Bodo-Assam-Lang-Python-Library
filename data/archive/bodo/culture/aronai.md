Aronai ek chhota scarf hai jise Bodo purush aur mahilayein dono use karte hain.
Wikipedia ke mutabiq yeh Bodo parampara ki pehchaan hai: ise atithiyon ka samman
karne aur uphaar dene ke liye use kiya jaata hai, sardiyon mein gale mein lapeta
jaata hai, aur Bodo nritya ke pradarshan mein aam taur par pehna jaata hai.
Bodoland Tourism ke mutabiq Dokhona aur jwmgra ke saath aronai Bodo mahilaon ki
sanskritik pehchaan hai.

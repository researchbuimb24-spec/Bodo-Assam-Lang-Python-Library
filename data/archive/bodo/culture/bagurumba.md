Bagurumba Bodo community ka sabse jaana-pehchana lok-nritya hai. Ise
"Butterfly dance" bhi kaha jaata hai. Yeh Bwisagu, yaani basant ke tyohaar,
ke samay kiya jaata hai. Isme Bodo mahilayein apna paramparik pehnawa pehenkar
nachti hain, aur sath mein Kham jaise vaadya bajte hain.

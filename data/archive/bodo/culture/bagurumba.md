Bagurumba Bodo samaj ka jaana-pehchana lok-nritya hai, jise Bodoland Tourism ne
sabse lokpriya nritya roop bataya hai aur "Butterfly dance" bhi kaha jaata hai.
Wikipedia ke mutabiq ise paramparik roop se Bodo log karte hain aur iske saath
Bagurumba geet gaaya jaata hai. Rooftop art-wiki ke mutabiq yeh Bodo mahilayein
Bwisagu ke dauran karti hain. Yeh vivran kamzor sources se hai aur kisi Bodo
bolne wale se confirm karna baaki hai.

Bwisagu Bodo samaj ka basant ka tyohaar hai, jo Bodo naye saal ki shuruaat
bhi maana jaata hai. Yeh Vishuba Sankranti ke aas-paas, April ke beech mein
manaya jaata hai. Isi tyohaar mein Bagurumba nritya aur Bwisagu ke geet gaaye
jaate hain.

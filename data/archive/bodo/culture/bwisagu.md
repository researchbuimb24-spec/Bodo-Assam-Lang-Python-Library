Bwisagu Bodo samaj ka basant ka tyohaar hai, jo Wikipedia ke mutabiq naye saal
ki shuruaat par mid-April mein manaya jaata hai. Rooftop art-wiki ise Vishuba
Sankranti ke aas-paas batata hai, jabki Anahad ke blog mein ise Baisakh mahine
(April-May) ka tyohaar likha hai. Bodoland Tourism ise Kherai aur Domashi ke
saath teen mukhya tyohaaron mein ginata hai. Sahi tareekh aur riwaaj kisi Bodo
bolne wale se confirm karna baaki hai.

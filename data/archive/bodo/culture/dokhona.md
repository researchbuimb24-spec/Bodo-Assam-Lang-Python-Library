Dokhona Bodo mahilaon ka paramparik aur mukhya pehnawa hai. Yeh ek lamba
aur aakarshak vastra hota hai jise chhati ke upar se lekar takhno (ankles)
tak lapetkar pehna jaata hai. Dokhona par haath se bune hue sundar phool aur
geometrical designs hote hain jinhe "Agor" kaha jaata hai, jaise Daorai Agor
ya Phool Agor. Bina Agor wale sadharan dokhona ko "Salamatha" kaha jaata hai,
jise dainik kaam ya puja-path ke samay pehna jaata hai.

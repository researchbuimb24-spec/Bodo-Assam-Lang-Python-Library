Dokhona Bodo mahilaon ka paramparik pehnawa hai. Wikipedia ke mutabiq yeh
lagbhag 3 meter lamba aur 1.5 meter chauda hota hai aur seene se pairon tak
lapeta jaata hai. Isme bunkar ke design (Agor) alag-alag rangon mein bune jaate
hain. Mukhya do prakaar bataye gaye hain: sada Dokhona aur design wala Dokhona.
Bina Agor wala "Matha Dokhona" puja ke samay pehna jaata hai, aur laal ya peele
rang ka Matha Dokhona dulhan ka paramparik pehnawa hai.

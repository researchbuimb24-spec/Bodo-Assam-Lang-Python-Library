Domashi (Magh Domashi) Bodo samaj ka fasal katne (harvest) ka mukhya utsav
hai, jo Magh mahine (January) ke madhya mein manaya jaata hai. Yeh Assamese
Magh Bihu ke samay hota hai. Is tyohaar par gaon ke log milkar paramparik
bhela-ghar aur meji jaise structures banate hain jinhe subah aag lagayi jaati
hai. Gharon mein vibhinn prakar ke pitha, laru aur paramparik pakwan bante
hain, tatha chhhote buzurgon ka aashirwad lete hain.

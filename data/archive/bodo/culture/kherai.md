Kherai Bodo community ka ek mukhya dharmik utsav hai, jisme gaana, nritya aur
dhol-baaje saath hote hain. Kherai ke antargat lagbhag 18 prakar ke nritya
maane jaate hain. Inhe Bathou ya Sibrai ki prarthana ke nritya kaha jaata hai.

Serja Bodo lok-sangeet ka ek paramparik taar-vaadya (stringed instrument) hai,
jo violin ya sarangi ke saman hota hai. Iska golaakar body lakdi aur chamde se
banta hai, jisme taar lage hote hain. Ise dhanush ke aakar ki kamani (bow) se
bajaya jaata hai. Serja ki madhur aur bhavuk dhun Bodo virah-geeton aur shant
lok-dhuno mein ras gholti hai.

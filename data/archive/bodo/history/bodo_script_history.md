Bodo bhasha ke liye lipi ka sawaal kai saal tak badalta raha. 1952 mein Bodo Sahitya Sabha ne tay kiya ki Assamese lipi ka upyog hoga, aur 1960 ke dashak mein Bodo mukhya roop se Assamese/Bengali lipi mein likhi gayi, jabki Isaai samudaay Latin ka upyog karta raha. Asamiya bhasha andolan ke dauran Bodo samudaay ko khatra mehsoos hua, aur 1970 mein Sabha ne apne 11ve varshik adhiveshan mein Latin lipi apnane ka faisla kiya. 1971 mein yeh maang Assam sarkar ne yeh kehkar thukra di ki Latin videshi lipi hai. Baad mein, Bharat sarkar ke saath hue samjhaute ke tahat Devanagari lipi ko apnaya gaya; ek source iski tareekh 9 April 1975 batata hai. Tab se Bodo ki aadhikarik lipi Devanagari hai. Exact tareekhein aur samjhaute ke vivran ko kisi aur sarkari ya vidwaan source se confirm karna baaki hai.
